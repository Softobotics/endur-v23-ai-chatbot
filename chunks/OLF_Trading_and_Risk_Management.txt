## Example Trade

Deal Capture and 
	 Operational Processing

On March 9th two parties, Party A and Party B, agree to a trade.  They 
	 decide to base a payment, to be made on December 15th, based on the 
	 price of Crude Oil on November 30.  They used the following 
	 rule to compute the payment:  For every dollar the price 
	 of crude is above $30 a barrel Party A gives Party B $10.  For 
	 every dollar the price of crude is below $30 Party B 
	 gives Party A $10.
