 Section heading: Having the Proper Rights to Assign Security Group Access.
 Text content: The owner of the task has the right to assign access to a task to specific security groups.  However, the task’s owner must also have rights to those groups. For example, a task owner must have access to the Front Office group to give it rights to run a task.  Otherwise, the owner will not see name of the group in the pick list from which the group is selected.
