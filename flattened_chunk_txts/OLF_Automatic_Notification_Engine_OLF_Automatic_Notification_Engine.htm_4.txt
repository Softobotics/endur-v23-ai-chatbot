 Section heading: How ANE Updates the Listing Part of the Trade Blotter.
 Text content: · Trades associated with portfolios for which the user is authorized. · Are of “type trading” (no authorization records or master instruments) · Any shared or perpetual trades that have a holding instrument configured on any of the input blotter pages.  The following items describe how the ANE update functions: · Single-page-only queries are supported. · A limited number of transaction table fields are matched.  (See the list below for the fields that are used for filtering updates from a query.) · Fields from tables other than transaction tables are not supported.  For example, transaction info, provisional, etc. · When a trade in the listing panel is modified and no longer matches the original query, it will remain on the blotter until a manual update occurs. · Matching criteria that are not supported are: - Complex wildcard matching such as "*match*this" - Combo queries like ">1+<5" in a single column.  (The workaround for this is to put ">1" and "+<5" into different columns.) .
