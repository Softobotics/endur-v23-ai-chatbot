 Section heading: Time Periods.
 Text content: For some date fields, the system allows you to define the date by entering a relative offset rather than a specific date. For example, if you enter 5y in the Maturity Date field on a deal entry window, the system calculates the maturity date to be 5 years from the start date.  Some fields require a relative offset.  In either case, when entering relative offsets in the system, enter a number followed by the letter d for days, w for weeks, m for months, and y for years.  The system incorporates good business day logic when using relative offsets to calculate future dates.
