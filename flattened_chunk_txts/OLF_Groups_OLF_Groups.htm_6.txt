 Section heading: How To: Deleting an Existing Group.
 Text content: To delete an existing group: 1. Click 
 the Select Group button and select the group to delete from the 
 picklist. The group’s name displays on the “Group Name” field. 2. To 
 check the items that belong to the group prior to deletion, click the List <Items> In Group button. The list displays all the items 
 defined for the particular group. 3. Click 
 the Delete Group button to delete the group. Note that items are 
 not deleted when groups are deleted. 4. Click 
 the OK button to save changes. The window closes and the column 
 of the group is removed from the Portfolio/Entity Groups window. .
