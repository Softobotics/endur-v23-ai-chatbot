 Section heading: Property 
 Settings.
 Text content: The Enable Market Data Incremental Processing property 
 has four possible settings. 1. <Please Choose> - 
 Default setting: This indicates that the service is not responding to 
 any market data changes. Feature is Off. 2. Indexes – This indicates 
 that the service is responding ONLY to Index changes. This includes any 
 price updates or any time an index is saved. 3. Volatilities & Correlations – This indicates that the service is responding ONLY to Volatility & 
 Correlation changes. This includes any input changes on the volatility/correlation 
 or any time the volatility/correlation is saved. 4. Indexes & Volatilities & 
 Correlations – This indicates that the service is responding to ALL 
 market data changes. This includes any input saves. This can put a significant 
 load on the system due to the number of potential updates. .
