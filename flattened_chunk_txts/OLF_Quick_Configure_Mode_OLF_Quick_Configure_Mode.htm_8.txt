 Section heading: Removing Fields from a Panel or the Blotter.
 Text content: To remove fields from the Grouping , Filter , or Pivot panel or the blotter, drag the field and drop it out of the panel/blotter or onto the Column Chooser .  To move it to another panel or to the blotter, drop it to the target panel or the blotter. .
