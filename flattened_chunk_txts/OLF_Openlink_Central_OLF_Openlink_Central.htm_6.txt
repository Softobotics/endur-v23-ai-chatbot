 Section heading: OpenLink Central Console Modes.
 Text content: The OpenLink Central Console can be displayed in three different modes: Full , Standard and Compact . Full Mode includes the Menu Bar , Tabs/Buttons, and the Title Bar . Standard Mode , the default mode when OpenLink Central is launched, does not contain the Menu Bar. Compact Mode only displays the Title Bar..
