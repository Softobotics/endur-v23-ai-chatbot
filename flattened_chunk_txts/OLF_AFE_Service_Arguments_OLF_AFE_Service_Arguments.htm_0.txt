 Section heading: Overview.
 Text content: The OpenLink Central Config: Service Arguments window is used to pass service arguments to OpenLink Services. Service Arguments facilitate the: · Association of an OpenLink Central Service with a specific service definition using OpenLink Central Configurations (e.g., OpenLink Desktop with “My Desktop”) · Configuration of an AFE (Adaptive Front End) Service for OpenLink Central as a process to open screens directly · Employment of AFE (Adaptive Front-End) Desktop Services filtered pick lists for OpenLink Central buttons.
