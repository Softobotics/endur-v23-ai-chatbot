 Section heading: Terminology.
 Text content: The following table contains a series of terms and definitions for 
	 trading and risk management, as they apply to the Open Link System..
 Table data: 
| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Trading | Deal Capture and Operational 
			 Processing. |
| Deal Capture | This term indicates the ability to model 
			 deal types and support deal entry. |
| Model Deal Types | The system must record all attributes 
			 of a deal, such as the price, quantity, counterparty, start 
			 and end dates, and many more fields related to each trade. |
| Deal Entry | Deals can be entered manually (by traders) 
			 or automatically (via trade import processes). |
| Operational Processing | Operational processing starts once deals 
			 are captured.  The system sends a confirmation to 
			 the counterparty that describes all of the deal details.  The 
			 counterparty reviews the confirmation and acknowledges the 
			 details of the trade.  Since trades have future 
			 payments tied to future events, the system will record the 
			 future event, compute the payment, generate an invoice, and 
			 satisfy any accounting requirements. |
| Risk Management | Risk Management means understanding 
			 your market risk, your exposure to price movements, from position 
			 management to the more advanced concept of Value-at-Risk (VaR). |
| Position Management | The system produces a consolidated position 
			 report for all the deals in the system to give a measure of 
			 your market risk. |
| Value-at-Risk | Value-at-Risk is a measure of your market 
			 risk condensed into one easy to understand number.  Value-at-Risk 
			 answers the question, "under the worst case scenario, 
			 what is the highest dollar amount that can be lost?” |.
