 Section heading: Removing an Existing Tab in OpenLink Central.
 Text content: To remove a tab from the OpenLink Central Console : 1. Select Tab Configuration . 2. Highlight the tab to delete in the “Available Tabs” list. 3. Click the Delete Tab button. The tab is removed from the list. 4. Click the OK button to accept the changes and close the window..
