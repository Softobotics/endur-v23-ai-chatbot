| <Toolbar Panel Name> | The toolbar panels that display depend on the page (or tab) from which theToolbar Area Configuration Menuwas accessed. All active user-defined (if any) and OLF-defined toolbar panels defined for the current active page or tab will be listed as options.For example, in V8.0r1 of the Settlement Desktop, if this menu was accessed from theCreate and Manage Documentstab, the available toolbar panel options are “SD Toolbar” and “SD Create_Manage Toolbar.” If this menu was accessed from theOutput Managementtab, the available toolbar panel options are “SD Toolbar” and “SD Output Mgmt Toolbar.”This is a toggle to show or hide the toolbar panel.
