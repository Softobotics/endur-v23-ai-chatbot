 Section heading: Adding a Service to OpenLink Central.
 Text content: To add a service that can be launched from an OpenLink Central Console tab: 1. Select the Button Configuration tab. 2. Select the tab where the service will be added in the “Tab Name” field. 3. Highlight the service that will be selected in the “Available Services” list, more than one service may be selected. The “Available Services” list may be reduced by selecting a Group Filter or entering a string in the search field. 4. Click the right-pointing arrow button. The service displays in the “Selected Services” list. 5. To reorder the “Selected Service” list, highlight the service to move and click the down arrow or up arrow button. 6. To enter a service argument, click the Args button. The OpenLink Central Config: Service Arguments window displays. Enter the argument and click the OK button. For example, if you are adding your “named” desktop in OpenLink Central, enter the name of the desktop in this screen. If your desktop was saved as “My Desktop”, enter “My Desktop” in this screen. 7. Enter the button caption and tool tip in the “Button Caption” and “Tool Tip Text” fields, respectively. 8. Select the icon for the service from the “Button Icon” field. The selected icon displays in the Icon Preview panel. 9. Click the OK button. The icon is now added to the selected OpenLink Central tab. To launch the service, click the icon..
