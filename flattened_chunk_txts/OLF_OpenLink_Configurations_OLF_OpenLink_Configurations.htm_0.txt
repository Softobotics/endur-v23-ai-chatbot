 Section heading: Overview.
 Text content: The OpenLink Central Configuration window allows users to add, remove and configure tabs and buttons (services) available in the OpenLink Central Console . Existing tabs may be deleted or renamed and new tabs may be created. AFE (Adaptive Front-End) Desktop services may also be added and removed using this window. The View Configuration tab was added in V12.1 and above. This tab is used to configure the appearance of the OpenLink Central Console when it is accessed from an OpenLink Central Sidebar. Selections made in this tab do not impact the configuration of the console..
